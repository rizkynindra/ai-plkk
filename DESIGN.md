# EPLKK UI direction

Audience: insurance claims staff processing hospital invoices and PKS tariff books.

Identity: a paper dossier on a desk, not a SaaS marketing site. The screen should feel like filing a claim folder.

Palette (2 cores + 1 accent):
- Paper: `#f3ece1`
- Ink: `#1c1914`
- Stamp red (accent, primary action only): `#9b2c2c`

Type: Georgia / `ui-serif` for page titles (document). System UI sans for forms, nav, and tables (readability at small sizes).

Radius: 4px inputs, 6px panels. No pills.

Elevation: one working panel may sit slightly off the paper. Nav is flat.

Motion: hover/focus only. No loops.

Layout: top nav with three real destinations. On small screens the links stack under a Menu button.
